Elements can sit inside a morph.

#### New

- Element children — an icon, avatar or badge enters, exits and travels with the words around it, tracked by its key.
- Inline formatting — a word can gain or lose `<strong>`, `<em>` or `<code>` and morph through the change.
- Interactive children — a button or a link inside a morph stays focusable and announced.
- `wrap` — lines fall where the width puts them, not only where the value has a newline.
- `TextMorph.update()` accepts an element to read as well as a string or a number.
